# Data Sources

## OECD — AI adoption

**Dataset:** ICT Access and Usage by Businesses  
**Indicator:** Businesses using artificial intelligence (AI), enterprises with 10+ employees  
**Source:** Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)  
**URL:** https://data-explorer.oecd.org/  
**API used in the working dataset:** https://sdmx.oecd.org/public/rest/data/OECD.STI.DEP,DSD_ICT_B@DF_BUSINESSES,1.0/OECD.A.G14_B...S_GE10?startPeriod=2020&dimensionAtObservation=AllDimensions

## CSET / Stanford HAI Global AI Vibrancy — publications

**Indicator:** AI conference publications  
**URL:** https://atlas.ginc.org/metrics/ai_conference_publications/

## CSET / Stanford HAI Global AI Vibrancy — patents

**Indicator:** AI patent grants  
**URL:** https://atlas.ginc.org/metrics/ai_patent_grants/

## Stanford AI Index 2025 — private AI investment

**Indicator:** Private AI investment, 2024  
**Source:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence / Quid  
**URL:** https://hai.stanford.edu/assets/files/hai_ai_index_report_2025.pdf

Only explicitly reported countries were populated in the assembled dataset. Missing entries must not be interpreted as zero investment.

## Ember — electricity indicators

**Dataset:** Yearly Electricity Data  
**Indicators used:** carbon intensity of electricity generation; renewable electricity share  
**URL:** https://ember-energy.org/data/yearly-electricity-data/

## Our World in Data / international energy sources — energy intensity

**Indicator:** Energy intensity of economies  
**Underlying sources:** IEA, IRENA, United Nations Statistics Division, World Health Organization, World Bank  
**URL:** https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/energy-intensity-of-economies?tab=table

The latest broadly comparable year used in this study is 2022.

## Important reuse note

This repository harmonizes variables obtained from third-party providers. Original data remain subject to the licenses, terms of use, attribution requirements, and redistribution rules of the respective providers. Users should consult those providers before redistributing source-derived data.
